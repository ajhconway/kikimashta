Scheduling

Adaptive scheduling uses ts-fsrs (MIT) and srs-kit (BSD-3-Clause). Their full license texts are included in the dependency notices. The initial model is FSRS-6 with shared parameters; personal parameter fitting will follow sufficient review history.

Japanese writing fonts

Writing practice uses Noto Sans JP and Noto Serif JP by Google/Adobe, Zen Maru Gothic by Yoshimichi Ohira / the Zen Maru Gothic Project Authors, and Klee One by Fontworks. These fonts are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License, version 1.1. Full licenses: Noto Sans JP, Noto Serif JP, Zen Maru Gothic, and Klee One. Provider-generated subsets cover the current word bank, with a whole-question Noto Sans JP fallback when Zen Maru Gothic lacks the ～ character; source URLs, byte hashes, and glyph coverage are recorded in writing-font provenance.

Japanese audio

Practice and vocabulary-card speech is AI-generated with ElevenLabs, model Eleven v4. The project owner selected Otani, Morioki, Goro, Tsuki, Takeshi, Lime, Ritsuto, and Itsuki through word and sentence auditions. Every card has a recording in each voice. See the voice registry and generation and processing provenance.

Delivery recordings receive constant-gain volume matching toward −22 LUFS with true-peak headroom. Clips constrained by peaks or short-utterance measurement remain quieter and are listed for review. Original speech timing and provider recordings are preserved. New word pronunciation, regional accents, and playback still need listening review on the iPhone.

Latin American Spanish audio

Travel levels 1 and 2 use AI-generated ElevenLabs Eleven v4 speech with eight selected regional voices: Juan and Pamela (Mexico City), Albeiro and Carito (Bogotá), Santiago and Agustina (Buenos Aires), and Carlos Figueroa and Veronica (Puerto Rico). Recordings use constant-gain volume matching with provider originals preserved. The owner accepted both listening reviews; real-iPhone playback acceptance remains separate. See the accepted media and provenance for Travel level 1 and Travel level 2.

Brand artwork

Brand artwork supplied by the project owner. The transparent cat mascot and cleaned icon were adapted with OpenAI’s built-in image editor. The supplied original is preserved; full edit prompts and asset records are in brand provenance.

Home shows Kiku expressions for the current review intensity. The expressions were made with the built-in image editor while preserving the original character. Retired generated variants are recoverable from Git history. Prompts and file hashes for retained variants are in Kiku intensity artwork provenance.

Pictures

Approved replacement illustrations were generated with OpenAI’s built-in image tool and delivered as compressed JPEGs. Source and delivery hashes, prompts and approval records for current artwork remain in the repository. See the active artwork provenance and its linked batch manifests.

The expanded vocabulary library contains original AI-authored SVG scenes and symbols under AGPLv3 only, plus reused starter artwork. Context-dependent pictures are flagged for review. Generator source, scene descriptions, asset hashes, and license records are in catalog image provenance.
