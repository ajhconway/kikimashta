Japanese audio

Practice speech is AI-generated with OpenAI, model gpt-realtime-2.1-mini, using the Marin and Cedar voices chosen through word and sentence comparisons. Every starter word has both recordings. Clips have gentle volume balancing, 120 ms of lead padding, and 240 ms of tail padding; no speech is trimmed. See practice audio provenance.

The voices have been chosen by the project owner. Pronunciation, pitch accent, and playback of the complete starter bank still need listening review on the iPhone.

Earlier Open JTalk audio

Speech synthesized with Open JTalk 1.11 and HTS Voice NIT ATR503 M001 1.05, HTS Working Group. Voice copyright 2003–2012 Nagoya Institute of Technology and 2003–2008 Tokyo Institute of Technology, licensed under CC BY 3.0. The clips have been encoded as MP3, with outside silence trimmed and short padding added.

These earlier baseline clips are retained for reference and are no longer used in practice. See earlier audio provenance.

Voice comparison

The separate voice comparison uses AI-generated Japanese speech from OpenAI, model gpt-realtime-2.1-mini, with the Marin, Cedar, and Coral voices. Gain and silence padding were applied before MP3 encoding. See audition provenance.

The full-sentence comparison uses Marin and Cedar. Generation and processing records are in sentence provenance.

Brand artwork

Brand artwork supplied by the project owner. The transparent cat mascot and cleaned icon were adapted with OpenAI’s built-in image editor. The supplied original is preserved; full edit prompts and asset records are in brand provenance.

Pictures

The six current picture cards are original, AI-authored SVG illustrations created for Kikimashta. They retain their separate MIT license. Their design records are in vector provenance.

The earlier watercolor illustrations and their generation records are preserved in the repository. They were generated with OpenAI's image generation tool; they are no longer the current picture cards.
