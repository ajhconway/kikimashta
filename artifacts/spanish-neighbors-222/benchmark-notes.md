# Independent Spanish neighbor benchmark (#222)

The benchmark was hand-authored from the frozen 159-word bank before inspecting either semantic model's outputs, phonemizer output or any generated rankings. No human listening preferences were collected or invented. The bank digest binds the referenced IDs to the exact approved texts, English labels, pack identities, pictures, exclusions and existing audio metadata.

## Semantic evaluation

The 32 strict ordering expectations contain 23 multiword-target rows, five ordinary-noun rows and four intended-sense rows. Every multiword entry in the frozen ordinary bank appears as a target. Related and unrelated item IDs are hand-selected broad contrasts. Higher cosine similarity for the related item is expected; report ties, reversals and unsupported items separately rather than converting deterministic list tie-breaking into successes. Category totals matter because phrase rows outnumber other rows. These expectations are content diagnostics, not empirically validated human similarity scores.

The four intended-sense rows address limón (Lime), banca (Bench), pollo (Chicken, food) and enchufe (Power outlet). The bank has no separate lemon or live-chicken item, so the lime and chicken ordering checks cannot themselves establish that an embedding resolved the full sense distinction. Inspect complete neighbor lists and the known-positive query neighborhoods too. The banca check contrasts seating against ATM to expose a financial-domain mismatch. The outlet/charger relation is a semantic positive with a separately recorded picture collision.

The twelve query sets provide selected known-useful neighbors for recall at five. They are deliberately incomplete: unlisted neighbors are not automatically wrong. Score unfiltered semantic rankings first. For picture-filtered reports, identify expected positives removed by validity rules and adjust/report the eligible denominator explicitly; do not label a correct semantic relation a failure merely because the approved picture pair is ambiguous. The six picture invariants include excluded pairs and an allowed food pair to keep validity separate from semantic closeness.

The predeclared comparison is Spanish fastText token composition versus multilingual E5-base with intended meaning. FastText uses unit-normalized word vectors, dropping only the fixed tokens de/del/la/el/los/las/y/en, then mean-pools and renormalizes. This is a transparent phrase baseline, not a claim that composition resolves idioms or static word senses. Preserve written diacritics. E5 input is `query: {Spanish surface} — {English label}` using the same frozen meanings, with pinned model and preprocessing. Both methods receive information that overlaps the intended-sense benchmark; an E5 win is not independent evidence that meaning was discovered from Spanish surface text alone. Select one eventual production method only after comparison and review.

## Pronunciation probes

The twenty probes exercise canonical Unicode equivalence, silent h, b/v equivalence, Latin American seseo, the selected yeísmo policy, contextual soft-g/j, primary stress, tap/trill, ñ, vowel sequences and multiword boundaries. The baseline is normalized letter edit distance; the candidate uses pinned eSpeak-NG es-419 phones while retaining primary stress and lexical word boundaries. No Japanese acoustic table, mora policy or 0.5 cutoff is applied.

Same-pronunciation expectations are conditional on the declared canonical dialect and isolated-word context. They are intended homophone-key checks, not labels for acoustic confusability across the app's four regional voice pairs. Some probes use real words outside the active bank; others, such as joined phrase spellings, are explicit technical probes only. None enter practice content or generate new speech. Report normalization/boundary invariants separately from exact phonetic-identity checks and exploratory inspections.

For río/frío, inspect that primary stress remains on i and the i/o vowel sequence is retained; the pair's distinctness alone does not verify a full timing or syllabification model. For casa/queso, the requested inspection checks the qu rule beyond merely asserting different output. Boundary probes require inspecting the emitted word segmentation, not inferring success only from input spaces. A joined spelling can have the same concatenated phone sequence as a phrase in continuous speech, so boundary preservation does not assert an acoustic contrast. Language switches, unsupported phones and word-count mismatches must be visible rather than silently repaired.

The canonical es-419 transcription cannot establish how each approved recording realizes ll/y or coda s. The llave/yave probe tests the selected merger with a controlled spelling, while listening across regions is still needed. Stress-only distinctions remain different keys; vowel accents and ñ must not be indiscriminately stripped as an ASCII-normalization shortcut.

Balance method-to-A/B assignment across the four voice passes for each target, freeze the order/seed, and use one common assigned voice for the target and both alternatives in a trial. Candidate presentation order must not expose rank.

Retain A, B, tie, neither and uncertain responses; null stays pending. Scope all alternatives to the approved ordinary Spanish picture pool and the selected pack, enforcing duplicate-image and pairwise exclusions. Nine direct foundation contexts remain out of the pool. Pin the actual audio bytes before collecting judgments.

A single owner's repeated judgments across words/voices are not independent participants. Report counts by region, missingness and reveal status; this small review does not establish statistical superiority, learner confusion or retention improvement.
