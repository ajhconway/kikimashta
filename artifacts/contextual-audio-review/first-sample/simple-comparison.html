Simple → contrastive

Three proposed simple contexts, six questions, and the existing Otani and Morioki recordings for comparison.

The simple tier uses one short event or fact. The contrastive tier asks learners to keep multiple facts straight. Both include a target-word question and a question about another detail.

This author review shows the text, meanings, choices and correct answers. Learners will hear Japanese first, with Japanese text and English revealed after answering.

Stage progression will use a correct first answer in a scheduled review with an FSRS next interval strictly greater than seven days. Each stage and answer format keeps its own schedule.

Content review notes

Simple: One ongoing event introduces direct questions before the matching contrastive context. The target occurrence 走っています is an inflection of catalogue 走る. Choices remain written because clear matching pictures are not available for every action or person. The detail question uses subject が without a relative clause.

Contrastive: The target occurrence is 走っていて, an inflection of catalogue 走る. Action choices keep the same progressive form and stay written because matching clear pictures do not exist for every action. The relative-clause question is within later Genki I. 走っていて explicitly carries the ongoing action into the joined clause.
