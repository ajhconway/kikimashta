# Two short contexts per level-1 word

37 words · 74 prompts · 69 shared card concepts · 296 answer slots.

Draft review: 56 existing images and 13 explicitly planned shared images. Existing image approval states are preserved. No new audio or artwork generation, production activation or progress changes.

The goal is to hear each target in a simple sentence or question. Direct picture matching is enough. Every card appears at least twice; shared cards keep the same identity and source bytes across words and correct/distractor roles.

## 力いっぱい（ちからいっぱい）

With All One's Strength

### 1

力いっぱい押してください。
ちからいっぱいおしてください。
Please push with all your strength.

どう押しますか。
どうおしますか。
How should you push?

Shared set: `effort`

- Pulling the box (`pull`)
- Carrying the box (`carry`)
- **Correct:** Pushing with all your strength (`push-hard`)
- Pushing gently with one finger (`push-gently`)

### 2

力いっぱい箱を押します。
ちからいっぱいはこをおします。
I push the box with all my strength.

Shared set: `effort`

- Carrying the box (`carry`)
- **Correct:** Pushing with all your strength (`push-hard`)
- Pushing gently with one finger (`push-gently`)
- Pulling the box (`pull`)

## 女（おんな）

Woman

### 1

女の人がいます。
おんなのひとがいます。
There is a woman.

Shared set: `woman`

- **Correct:** Woman character (`woman`)
- Man character (`man`)
- Two cats (`cats`)
- Two babies (`babies`)

Review note: 女 is used in the common beginner phrase 女の人. These are designated characters; clothing/hair do not define gender.

### 2

あそこに女の人がいます。
あそこにおんなのひとがいます。
There is a woman over there.

Shared set: `woman`

- Man character (`man`)
- Two cats (`cats`)
- Two babies (`babies`)
- **Correct:** Woman character (`woman`)
