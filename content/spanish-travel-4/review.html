supermercado

Supermarket

Current picture concept and notes

Recolour the same supermarket with a clear GREEN AND WHITE theme: rich leaf-green roof trim, green-and-white striped awning, white/cream walls, green shopping carts and green shelf accents. Let green lead instead of purple, pink and gold; use restrained yellow highlights only where helpful. Preserve the existing storefront layout, large shopping-cart pictogram, foreground cart and shelves of packaged groceries. Keep the recognizable friendly rounded cartoon shapes and dark green/deep-violet contours. Do not add lettering, logos, fruit piles or new objects.

Keep distinct from the open-air market and bakery; do not add fruit piles as the primary subject.

Current picture concept and notes

Change the colour treatment of this SAME post office to a clear RED AND WHITE postal theme: rich red roof trim, red-and-white awning, white/cream walls, red mail box and white envelope pictograms. Retain subtle deep-purple or dark-red contours, but let red and white lead. No lettering, flags or postal-company logo.

Use an envelope symbol, not text; avoid a generic shop that depends on a label.
