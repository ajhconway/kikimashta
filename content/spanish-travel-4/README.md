## Wording

Camión de carga explicitly means cargo truck; camión alone can mean bus in Mexico. Llanta depicts the rubber tire, patrulla depicts a police vehicle, and limón depicts a green lime. Curita uses the common adhesive-bandage sense without a brand logo. Reloj de pulsera and tienda de campaña disambiguate wristwatch and tent. Calcetín/guante are singular; tijeras/binoculares refer to one pair. All eight voices use the same chosen words.
