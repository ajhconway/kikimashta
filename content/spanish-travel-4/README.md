## Wording

Camión de carga explicitly means cargo truck; camión alone can mean bus in Mexico. Llanta depicts the rubber tire, patrulla depicts a police vehicle, and limón depicts a green lime. Curita uses the common adhesive-bandage sense without a brand logo. Reloj de pulsera and tienda de campaña disambiguate wristwatch and tent. Calcetín/guante are singular; tijeras/binoculares refer to one pair. All eight voices use the same chosen words.

## Lexical references

- [ASALE: camión (Mexican bus sense)](https://www.asale.org/damer/cami%C3%B3n): Use camión de carga to distinguish the proposed truck from the existing autobús card.
- [ASALE: llanta](https://www.asale.org/damer/llanta): Rubber tire sense explicitly includes Mexico and many American varieties.
- [ASALE: patrulla](https://www.asale.org/damer/patrulla): Police vehicle sense explicitly includes Mexico and many American varieties.
- [ASALE: curita](https://www.asale.org/damer/curita): Adhesive strip sense used across many American varieties; no brand is drawn.
