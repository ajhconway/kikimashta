## Spanish adaptive distractors: audit and next milestone (#222)

Spanish still uses ordinary randomized word alternatives; its semantic/pronunciation integration is tracked in [#222](https://github.com/ajhconway/kikimashta/issues/222). This first audit covers the actual five-bank ordinary picture pool in `src/core/languages.ts`: 159 unique words, 23 multiword expressions, 29 spellings with acute accents, seven with ñ and none with ü. All 159 lack explicit reading/IPA fields and have eight existing recordings each (1,272 ordinary-word clips). The nine direct foundation contexts are separate and must not be admitted as ordinary distractor words. Picture validity currently combines 59 global exclusion pairs and the bank-local carro/taxi pair: 60 unique effective pairs.

The first offline semantic candidate is the official [Spanish fastText model](https://fasttext.cc/docs/en/crawl-vectors.html), with explicitly declared token-vector composition for phrases such as tarjeta de crédito and estación de policía. Compare intended-sense and phrase behavior before selecting production data; do not assume the Japanese result transfers. The comparison uses [multilingual E5-base](https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-base) with explicit intended meanings, matching the established Japanese experiment family rather than the initially proposed small variant. Define the benchmark before examining rankings, covering phrases and ambiguous entries such as limón (Lime), banca (Bench), pollo (the pictured cooked food), and enchufe (wall outlet). Pin model/source hashes, licenses and the exact input/meaning recipe, retaining one production semantic method after evaluation.

For pronunciation, first evaluate pinned [eSpeak-NG es-419 phonemization](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/blob/master/espeak-ng-data/lang/roa/es-419) with lexical stress and phone/word boundaries preserved. [Phonemizer's options](https://bootphon.github.io/phonemizer/cli.html) require explicit stress and boundary settings. Start with normalized phoneme edit distance; describe it as phonetic, not acoustically calibrated. [PanPhon articulatory features](https://aclanthology.org/C16-1328/) are a possible later weighting comparison, not validated Spanish listener-confusion costs. Do not reuse Japanese mora units, acoustic weights or the Japanese 0.5 eligibility gate without Spanish evaluation.

Benchmark existing stress/hiatus forms café, cámara, río, frío and azúcar, with clearly labeled controlled probes such as papa/papá and canto/cantó. Audit homophones using supported pronunciation varieties, including [silent h](https://www.rae.es/dpd/h), [b/v](https://www.fundeu.es/recomendacion/bvw-pronunciacion/), [seseo](https://www.rae.es/dpd/seseo) and the chosen [yeísmo policy](https://www.rae.es/dpd/ye%C3%ADsmo). These are review cases, not assertions of collisions already present in the bank. Review ll/y and coda-s examples against the actual Mexico City, Bogotá, Buenos Aires and Puerto Rico voices; one canonical transcription may not capture all regional realizations. Preserve accents rather than stripping them as a spelling shortcut.

The [frozen offline evaluation](../artifacts/spanish-neighbors-222/README.md) covers all 159 words. FastText composition passes 31/32 independent ordering hypotheses versus 28/32 for the tested E5-base recipe. ESpeak-NG passes 19/20 transcription probes; the halla/haya canonical yeísmo mismatch blocks pronunciation activation. Future comparisons should avoid mandatory four-voice repetition. No pronunciation method is selected by these preferences.
